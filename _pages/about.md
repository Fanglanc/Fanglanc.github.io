Fanglan Chen holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Virginia Tech, where she was advised by Dr. [Chang-Tien Lu](https://people.cs.vt.edu/~clu/). She also holds an M.S. in Computer Science and an MURP in Urban and Regional Planning from Virginia Tech. Her research bridges predictive modeling, graph representation learning, and combinatorial optimization, advancing deep learning methods, especially graph neural networks and reinforcement learning, for urban computing problems. She also builds applied machine learning systems spanning multimodal VLM/LLM pipelines, retrieval-augmented generation, document classification, and uncertainty-aware evaluation to enable reliable automation, confidence scoring, and human-in-the-loop decision workflows. She has published in top-tier venues including AAAI, KDD, ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, and ECCV, with her work on automated school redistricting featured by [AIhub](https://aihub.org/2023/05/02/interview-with-fanglan-chen-exploring-tradeoffs-in-automated-school-redistricting/). She serves on program committees such as AAAI and PAKDD and is interested in shipping scalable AI services that reconcile data and documents into trusted sources of truth for high-stakes decision-making.
