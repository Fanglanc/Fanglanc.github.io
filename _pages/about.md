Fanglan Chen holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science from Virginia Tech, where she was advised by Dr. [Chang-Tien Lu](https://people.cs.vt.edu/~clu/). She also holds an M.S. in Computer Science and an MURP in Urban and Regional Planning from Virginia Tech. Her research bridges predictive modeling, graph representation learning, and combinatorial optimization, advancing deep learning methods, especially graph neural networks and reinforcement learning, for large-scale forecasting, optimization, and decision problems. She also builds applied machine learning systems spanning multimodal VLM/LLM pipelines, retrieval-augmented generation, document classification, and uncertainty-aware evaluation, with experience developing end-to-end workflows for data processing, model development, benchmarking, and reliable decision support. Her research has been published in leading venues including AAAI, KDD, ACL, EMNLP, NAACL, and ECCV, and her work on automated school redistricting was featured by [AIhub](https://aihub.org/2023/05/02/interview-with-fanglan-chen-exploring-tradeoffs-in-automated-school-redistricting/). She has served on program committees including AAAI and PAKDD and is interested in building scalable, reliable AI and analytical systems that combine machine learning, optimization, and structured data to support complex analytical decision-making.
